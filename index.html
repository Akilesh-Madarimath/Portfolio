About me
Bengaluru, India
Mission

Turn messy, high-volume data into models that catch what manual review misses — without burying the team in false positives.

Vision

Become the analyst teams trust to translate a confusion matrix into a decision the business can actually act on.

I'm a Computer Science graduate (2025) who builds Python-based machine learning systems, with a specific pull toward fraud, waste, and abuse detection.

Most of my hands-on work sits at the intersection of statistical modeling and applied ML: building multi-classifier pipelines (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, SVM), handling imbalanced data with SMOTE, and tuning precision-recall trade-offs so a model's threshold actually matches what the business can tolerate. I work primarily in Python — Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras, Pandas, NumPy — backed by SQL and NoSQL data modeling in MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB.

Alongside the modeling work, I've picked up NLP and prompt engineering — TF-IDF vectorization and natural language prompt development for LLM applications — and I treat data privacy and discretion as part of the job, not an afterthought, especially when handling sensitive datasets for stakeholders.

My foundation is a B.Tech in Computer Science from Jain College of Engineering & Technology, with coursework spanning machine learning, databases, operating systems, and software engineering. What stands out across my internships and projects is a habit: I don't ship a model without quantifying it first — F1, ROC-AUC, precision-recall — and without writing it up clearly enough that a non-technical stakeholder can act on it.
